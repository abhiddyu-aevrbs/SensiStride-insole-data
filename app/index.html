Setup & troubleshooting

Requirements

  • Chrome or Edge on desktop, or Chrome on Android. iPhone is not supported (no Web Bluetooth on iOS).
  • Page must be served over HTTPS or localhost.
  • Firmware: install the ArduinoBLE library, flash DFU_Insole_BLE.ino. It advertises as DFU Insole.

Common problems

SymptomFix
Connect does nothingWrong browser or not on HTTPS/localhost.
Device not listedNot powered, or another device is already connected (BLE allows one at a time).
Connects then dropsLow battery or loose USB. Recharge, move closer.
Two sensors read the sameSolder bridge. Re-run the build guide continuity tests.
Channel stuck at 4095 / 04095 = shorted to 3.3V or missing pull-down. 0 = open sensor or shorted to ground.
Wrong sensor respondsCable in wrong port. Ball→A1, Big Toe→A2, Heel→A3, Little Toe→A0.
